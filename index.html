ABOUT THE SCHOOL

Our Story

Discover the history and values of Aggrey Memorial.

A legacy of learning

Founded in 1940, Aggrey Memorial A.M.E. Zion Senior High School is located at Moree Hill (Brafo Yaw), Cape Coast, Ghana.

The school’s name honours Dr. James Emman Kwegyir Aggrey, celebrated for his contribution to education. The school seeks to develop students academically and in character.
